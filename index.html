Regional swings

Margin Δ moves every county in a region toward D (+) or R (−) by that many points; turnout Δ is points of eligible voters. Shifts apply to every office and stack on all other inputs. Regions overlap (South and Border, Midwest and Rust Belt), and where they overlap the shifts add. “Now (start)” is the region’s simulated margin against the starting election. The border region is the 22 counties touching Mexico (it needs county data, and includes big counties like San Diego, El Paso and Pima). Scenario sizes are illustrative, not forecasts.
